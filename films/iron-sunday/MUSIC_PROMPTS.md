# IRON SUNDAY — Music Cue Sheet (ElevenLabs Music v2.5)
Base template = the owner's own prompt that worked (2026-09-24). Every cue keeps its skeleton:
dusty analog 1960s mix, ~120 BPM galloping pulse, nylon-string verses, baritone twang + mariachi
trumpet duo, jaw harp, castanets, whistle, half-time choir-oohs break, mariachi/Tex-Mex swagger,
Rango desert-western energy. Only the mood and the lead colour change per cue. Never slow and moody.
Fade-friendly: steady sections, no dead stop, ends on a ringing chord. Set length manually, make two
versions. Never inside a video prompt.
