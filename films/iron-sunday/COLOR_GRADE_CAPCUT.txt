EP01 colour grade in CapCut desktop (owner 2026-09-25). Goal: one look from the first shot to the last.
Look: dusty spaghetti western, warm sun-bleached highlights, slightly cool shadows, low saturation,
the red scarf is the only strong red. Numbers are starting points; judge by eye on 3-4 different clips.

STEP 1 - FIX EACH CLIP FIRST (select the clip > Adjust > Basic)
Only fix clips that stand out (too bright, too dark, too blue, too orange). Use a well-matched clip as the
"hero" reference (e.g. a CH-02 church-yard wide) and nudge the others toward it:
- Exposure / Highlights / Shadows until brightness matches the hero.
- Temperature / Tint until the sand looks the same colour as in the hero.
Do NOT add the look here, only make clips match each other.

STEP 2 - ONE LOOK FOR THE WHOLE FILM (Adjustment layer)
Adjustment > Custom adjustment > drag onto a track ABOVE all clips > stretch it over the whole timeline.
Basic:
  Temperature +10    Tint +3        Saturation -15
  Contrast +12       Highlights -15 Shadows -8
  Whites -5          Blacks -10     Brilliance +5
  Sharpen 10         Vignette 15    Grain 15
Color wheels (if available): Shadows toward teal (small), Highlights toward orange (small),
  Midtones leave alone.
HSL:
  Red     saturation +10 (keeps the scarf strong)
  Orange  saturation 0, luminance +5 (sand and skin)
  Yellow  saturation -15
  Green   saturation -40 (nothing should look lush)
  Cyan/Blue saturation -25, hue slightly toward cyan (sky stays pale and dusty)
  Purple/Magenta saturation -30
Curves (optional): a gentle S-curve on RGB, and lift the very bottom point a little for a faded film black.
When happy: Save as preset ("ORGANIST LOOK") so Episode 2 uses the same grade.

STEP 3 - SCENES THAT NEED THEIR OWN LAYER
If a scene still looks different under the main layer (church interior, dam, radio-tower dust), add a
second short adjustment layer only over that scene and correct it (usually Exposure and Temperature)
until it sits next to the scenes around it. Keep the main layer on top of everything.

STEP 4 - CHECK
Scrub the whole film at low speed and watch the cuts between scenes: the sand colour, the sky colour and
the darkness of the shadows should not jump. The red scarf should always be the strongest colour.
Title card and black frames: exclude them from grain/vignette if they look muddy (cut the layer there).
