=== STYLE box
Spaghetti western theme, instrumental, steady 104 BPM from start to end, D minor. A wheezing old church pipe organ plays a dark, catchy five-note riff as the main hook, repeating over a steady galloping drum groove that never speeds up. Twangy baritone electric guitar with tremolo, nylon guitar strums, a lone whistle and a mariachi trumpet answering the organ, soft choir "ahh" pads, dusty 1960s analog mix, wide stereo, warm low end. Cool, dangerous, confident swagger. Same tempo, same rhythm and same energy the whole track, no build-up, no drop, no tempo change, no big finale; loop-friendly and easy to fade out at any point.
