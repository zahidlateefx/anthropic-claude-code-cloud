# IRON SUNDAY — Series Bible
Locked 2026-09-22. Film id: `iron-sunday`. Episode 1 target 7:20, 16:9.
SIX COFFINS stays shelved; nothing here supersedes it.

## 0. What this series is
A dialogue-light, creature-cast sci-fi spaghetti western. Fast, kinetic, high body count,
cut in the grammar of the reference channel (mean shot about 3.9 s, fight shots 1.2-2.5 s).
Every episode is a PURSUIT ACROSS THE FRONTIER, never a siege in one room.

## 1. HOOK
The gang buried their own contract-killer under the mission floor three years ago.
This Sunday the thing playing the organ in that mission is wearing an iron mask,
and it does not speak. Forty ride in. One walks out.

> Logline: *He never runs. He just never stops.*

## 2. LOCATION POLICY (the reason this series exists in this shape)
Location consistency is the project's biggest failure mode (owner, stated twice).
So: **every location is visited ONCE, for 40-60 seconds, then abandoned for the rest of the episode.**
A place the audience never returns to cannot be caught drifting.

- 8 locations per episode, scene change roughly every 45 s.
- Only TWO places recur across the series and therefore get a location reference image:
  the IRON MISSION exterior, and the CRYPT (the closing shot of every episode).
  Every other place is one-visit and gets NO location image — EXTEND carries the room forward.
- Within one visit: one master wide generated from refs, then mediums / close-ups / inserts,
  every following clip an EXTEND of the previous one.

## 3. WORLD
**THE PARCH** — a lawless off-world desert territory. Two pale moons visible by day.
No law and no town big enough to have any. Water is currency, kept in riveted iron tanks.
Everything is rusty, patched with scrap iron and tin, warped planks, bullet holes, cobwebs,
rubbish in the dust. Nothing is clean or new, ever.

**Colour policy (hard).** The world is desaturated dust, rust, bone and burnt black.
Only three saturated colours exist anywhere in the film:

- **blue** = water (only in ACT 7, the tank)
- **red** = blood and dynamite
- **amber** = the single oil lamp on the organ, and the bell-tower lantern

Nothing else is bright. No green, no purple, no grading toward teal.

## 4. THE CRYPT WALL — the series engine
Under the mission floor twelve names are cut into the stone: the twelve who put him in the ground.
Every episode ends on the same shot — the burn-scarred hand, the knife, one name scratched
through, the new count.

## 5. EPISODE FORMULA (6-8 min)
1. 45 s opening ride with credits (locked Version A, section 7).
2. The gang is found at a place of worship, work or trade. First exchange. The target runs.
3. Five to six travelling locations, each 40-60 s, each a self-contained set-piece that costs
   the gang men and costs the ORGANIST something real.
4. The duel. The target dies in a way the location made possible, never by a lucky shot.
5. 40 s crypt coda: the knife, the name, the count, "To be continued".

Fixed per episode: ONE running gag (the CHOIR copying the ORGANIST one beat late and wrong),
ONE real setback (a lost mount, weapon or Choir member), ONE step of the mystery.

## 6. CAST — locked designs, do not redraw

### RECURRING (these three carry all the consistency load)

**THE ORGANIST** — 2.2 m, very tall, very thin.
Scorched ankle-length black cassock, rust-pitted iron collar plate across the shoulders,
face completely hidden behind a riveted iron grille-mask with one narrow horizontal slit.
Bare burn-scarred four-fingered hands, no gloves. Dusty boots, no spurs.
Two sawn-off shotguns, hidden inside the organ at the start, afterwards crossed at his back.
**Never speaks, never makes a voice sound, never runs** — he walks while everyone else runs.
Pronoun policy: unlike the SIX COFFINS WELDER, the ORGANIST is openly "he".

**SISTER PIKE** — 1.5 m armadillo nun. Banded grey-brown plates down her back over a faded
black habit, white coif grey with dust, a belt of six red dynamite sticks at the waist,
a long iron key-chain in her right hand used as a flail. Short flat spoken lines only.

**THE CHOIR** — toad-gunmen, 0.7 m, wide flat olive heads, huge wet yellow eyes,
torn white-and-grey choir robes over gun belts, each with a different mismatched weapon.
Six in EP01; two survive and continue into the series. Comic chorus: heads turning in unison,
blinking in unison, copying the ORGANIST's gestures one beat late and wrong.

### EPISODE 1 ONLY

**BOSS CALDERA** — 2.4 m heavy gila-monster. Beaded black-and-orange hide, flat broad head,
flicking tongue, a grin of gold teeth, a heavy silver cross on a chain at the neck (looted from
the old parson), long grey duster, two long-barrel revolvers. Laughs constantly.

**CALDERA'S RIDERS** — nine mixed creature gunmen (rattlesnake, buzzard, boar, jackrabbit,
two lizards, three coyotes), 1.6-2.1 m, grey dusters, flat black hats, mismatched rifles and
sawn-offs. Disposable. Each gets a 1.5 s aim-at-lens portrait before dying.

### MOUNTS AND VEHICLES

**DUST-HORSE** — 2.2 m gaunt long-legged reptilian horse-creature, grey leathery hide, no mane,
bone ridges down the neck, split-claw hooves, worn western saddle and saddlebags.
The ORGANIST rides a black one; the riders' are grey and sand-coloured.

**HOVER-TRUCK** — 4 m rusty flatbed hover-truck, riveted boiler body, two glowing orange thruster
pods underneath, scrap-iron cab with no doors, water barrels roped to the bed. Two in EP01.

### SIZE ORDER
CHOIR 0.7 < SISTER PIKE 1.5 < riders 1.6-2.1 < DUST-HORSE 2.2 = ORGANIST 2.2 < CALDERA 2.4 < HOVER-TRUCK 4.0 long

## 7. OPENING — VERSION A (locked, owner re-confirmed 2026-09-22)
No cold open. The film starts on the ride.

- **SD-01A (15 s)** bright full-wide aerial drone over open desert, distant dust rising.
  The camera zooms slowly into the dust for 8-10 s until the convoy appears, then locks and tracks.
- **SD-01B (15 s)** side tracking, convoy at full speed, one angle change at about 8 s. Credits run low in frame.
- **SD-01C (15 s)** ground-level pass with the convoy crossing over the lens, then rear drone toward
  the mission, which appears on the horizon. Title.

Riders always at full speed; only the camera is slow. Max 2 angles per 15 s clip, no cut inside a
drone move. Bright, clean, open frame, no dark foreground, no rocks or caves in front of the lens.
Frame bottom and corners stay quiet for the owner's credits.

## 8. LOCKED SOUND TEXTS (verbatim in every Audio line where the thing appears)
- **HOVER-TRUCK**: *a deep diesel-generator drone with a rattling loose body panel, a hissing pressure valve, and a low bass thrum that rises and falls with distance*
- **THE ORGAN**: *a wheezing pipe organ with leaking bellows, two dead notes that click instead of sounding, and a long stone reverb tail*
- **THE BELL**: *a cracked bronze bell struck by bullets, each hit a different pitch with a long metallic shimmer*
- **DUST-HORSE**: *split-claw hooves hitting hard dry ground in a fast four-beat, leather saddle creak, snorting breath*

No background music in any clip. Every Audio line ends with `No background music.`

## 9. NOT ALLOWED
No humans. No on-screen text except in-world signage. No clean or new surfaces.
No single-location episodes. No returning to a one-visit location.
No music inside prompts. No "backward / retreating / reverse" in a camera line.
No similes, no "something / someone / off-screen / as if". No frame fractions.
One mover per shot; everyone else holds still or reacts once.
