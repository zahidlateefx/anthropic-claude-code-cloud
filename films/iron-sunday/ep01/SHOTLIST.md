# IRON SUNDAY — EP01 "Iron Sunday" — Shot List
Target cut 7:20. 72 generated clips, about 900 s of footage for 440 s of film.
MODE column: **NEW** = generate from reference images. **EXTEND** = feed the previous clip in and
re-attach the character sheets. Location images exist for only two places (see IMAGE_INDEX.md);
every other location is carried by the EXTEND chain and is never returned to.

Convention: each act opens with ONE master wide, then mediums / close-ups / inserts.
Never two consecutive wides of the same place. One mover per shot.

## Production order (recommended)
1. All reference images (IMAGE_INDEX.md), character sheets first, then the size chart, then props.
2. **SD-02** (Act 1 master, 30 s) as the first real test render — it has the most characters, the
   most sub-beats and the organ prop. If it holds, the whole EXTEND method is proven.
3. SD-01A opening ride (it must sell the film in 10 seconds; worth 3 generations).
4. Then act by act, master first, then its EXTEND chain.

## Risk notes, stated honestly
- The EXTEND chain has NOT been proven on a chain this long in this project. If a chain drifts after
  4-5 links, break it: generate a fresh NEW master mid-act from refs and continue extending from there.
- Nine riders will not stay identical. They are deliberately disposable and mostly seen in 1.5 s
  portraits and at speed; do not spend effort trying to lock them.
- Act 3 and Act 6 carry the camera-direction risk (vehicles). Never write "backward / retreating /
  reverse"; always describe the subject travelling forward, nose first, with dust behind the tail.
