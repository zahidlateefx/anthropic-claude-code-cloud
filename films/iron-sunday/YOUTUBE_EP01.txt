THE ORGANIST | Episode 1: Iron Sunday | Sci-Fi Western Short Film

=== DESCRIPTION
Three years ago, a gang of outlaws buried a man under the floor of a ruined desert church. This Sunday, someone is playing its old organ again.

Under two moons, the gang rides back to finish the job. Waiting for them at the organ is a stranger in a rusty iron mask, with two sawn-off shotguns and no words to waste.

Expect creature gunslingers, rusty hover-trucks, reptile riders, fast shotgun fights, and a masked stranger who never runs. Every outlaw he takes down leads him one step closer to the man on the radio.
