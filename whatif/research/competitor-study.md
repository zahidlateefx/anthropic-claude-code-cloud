## What they all share
- **Hook 0–4s**: serif question, centered. Scene already "normal" so the threat reads instantly.
- **HUD counter** top-left that never stops moving; the label changes as the story escalates
  (M87: distance → mass → width → light speed → time left).
- **Camera vocabulary** (not static): locked establishing → slow push-in → tilt up to follow the
  threat → shake + roll on impacts → a big final move (fall, dive, engulf, fly-in).
- **Climax**: white/orange flash, cut to dark, HUD value reads **HERE**, then end card.
- **End card**: title again + one true fact in tiny caps; sometimes "Tomorrow: …" teaser.
- **Loops** (growing, earth hole) end on the opening frame → rewatches.
- **Sound**: no voiceover/music track; ambience → riser → silence/hit → rumble; louder as it escalates;
  silence on the end card.

## How episode 01 goes beyond them
- Camera: 15 keyframed moves (push, pan to the sky, dolly, crane up, look up, dive through the ice

- Life in the scene: people on a dock who freeze, look up and run; birds; cars whose headlights stay on until
  the cars stop; city windows that switch on then fail building by building.

- Details: retina afterimage of the Sun, Milky Way, waves that freeze, spreading ice cracks, frosted railing.
- Sound: birds/crowd/traffic cut dead at 0:00, sub drop + tinnitus ring, gasp and footsteps, sirens and horns,
