# TEST PROJECT — Screenshot -> Nano Banana blocking reference
Owner's idea, 2026-09-24. Kept separate from IRON SUNDAY until it is proven. Nothing here is a rule
for the film yet.

## The question
Can a location + character-position reference image, built by Nano Banana Pro from screenshots of
clip 1, make Seedance keep the room and everyone's positions in clip 2?

## Method
1. **Clip 1 (TEST-01, 30 s)** enters a NEW location and deliberately shows it in several WIDE shots
   from different angles, so the room and every character's place are revealed. After the characters
   take their places, nobody moves again until the clip ends.
2. The owner takes screenshots from clip 1 (at least: the doorway wide, the reverse wide, the high
   wide) and pastes them in chat.
3. The director compares the screenshots with the video prompt and writes the Nano Banana prompt:
   - the room is rebuilt as one clear image from an angle that shows both sides of the room;
   - each character is placed by name, with his reference sheet attached;
   - **conflict rule:** if a character is in two places across screenshots, the position he holds at
     the END of clip 1 wins; the earlier position is not drawn;
   - **extra character rule:** anyone visible in a screenshot who should not be in the blocking (a
     passer-by, a duplicate, someone who has left) is named in the prompt and explicitly excluded;
   - where the screenshots contradict the plan for clip 2, the director corrects it in the image
     prompt and says so.
4. The owner generates the image (image B1).
5. **Clip 2 (TEST-02, 30 s)** tags B1 as the location-and-blocking reference and continues the scene.
6. Judge: did the room match? did everyone stay in their places? did anyone duplicate or vanish?

## Pass / fail
- PASS: room layout, character places and screen direction hold across the cut between TEST-01 and
  TEST-02. Then the method goes into the skill and wide shots become allowed in every 30 s clip.
- FAIL: record exactly what broke (room, positions, extra characters, first frame) before any retry.

## Cost control
TEST-01 reuses sheets that already exist (image 5 CALDERA, 6 ORGANIST, 8 FANG, 10 TUSK, 1 heights),
so the test needs NO new character images. Only the Nano Banana blocking image is new.
Four characters, the owner's maximum.
